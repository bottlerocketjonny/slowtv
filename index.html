About

Slow TV is a British indie/alternative band crafting raw, authentic soundscapes that capture the essence of modern DIY music culture.

Born from late-night rehearsals and a shared love of underground music scenes, we blend atmospheric guitars with driving rhythms to create something uniquely ours. Our sound is unpolished, honest, and unapologetically independent.

Current Lineup

  • Rob Edwards-Bell - Vocals, Guitar
  • Tom Beeton - Guitar
  • Euan Kearney - Bass Guitar
  • Jonny Coddington - Drums, BVs
